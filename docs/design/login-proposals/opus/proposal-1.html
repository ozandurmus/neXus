Evolves the X

The two-colour X stays (teal and blue, as today) but becomes a hub: four nodes joined through one central node. A nexus is a connection point; the mark now says so.

Wordmark

Drawn monoline on one 7-unit stroke. The n and the u mirror each other around the X, so the name is symmetric about its own hub.

Small sizes

At 20 px the nodes merge into a clean X on the navy tile. The hub ring is the only detail that must survive; it does at 16 px.
