# neXus Sign-In & Logo Proposals — Argon

## Proposal 1 (`proposal-1.html`) — Constellation Pulse
**Constellation Pulse** frames the sign-in screen as a dark-first operational command center (with an instant Light/Dark switch) where a live multi-vendor estate topology accompanies a high-contrast operator authentication console. The mood is calm, authoritative, and technical, using a lightweight 2D canvas animation that sends low-CPU verification pulses along corroboration edges between masked HA cluster peers (`CLS-ROMEO-01`, `FW-TANGO-04`, `FW-JULIET-06`, `FW-BRAVO-02` on `192.0.2.x`) and halts cleanly under `prefers-reduced-motion`. For the logo, this proposal **evolves the "X" motif** into the **Corroborated Vector X** (shown in both light and dark inline SVG variants): two interlocking teal and blue conduits converge on a central diamond verification core flanked by four endpoint nodes, paired with a custom geometric `neXus` wordmark. It fits neXus because independent peer corroboration and direct-device identity verification are the foundation of `SEE → VERIFY`.

## Proposal 2 (`proposal-2.html`) — Deterministic Trace
**Deterministic Trace** turns the sign-in screen into a crisp, blueprint-inspired packet and policy inspection studio (defaulting to high-contrast Light mode with a Dark mode toggle) built around the five product planes: `SEE → VERIFY → TRACE → RECOVER → OPERATE`. The mood is surgical and diagnostic, powered by pure CSS and inline SVG keyframe animations where packet capsules and dashed signal trains flow across five parallel operational lanes through rule-matching, drift-alignment, and CAS backup gates without any JavaScript timer loop. Rather than keeping the old cross, this proposal **introduces a new logo motif**—the **Five-Plane Conduit Shield** (rendered in light and dark variants)—which places five parallel circuit traces bending at 45° inside a chamfered hexagonal shield alongside a balanced typographic `neXus` wordmark. It fits neXus by visually unifying path analysis, policy hygiene, and the five-step evidence lifecycle right at the entry gate.

## Proposal 3 (`proposal-3.html`) — Bastion Grid
**Bastion Grid** presents a centered vault monolith flanked by symmetrically balanced telemetry and state-specification wings over an expansive isometric security matrix. The mood is fortified, banking-grade, and architectural, featuring an inline SVG isometric grid where segmented firewall zone prisms (`FW-TANGO-04`, `CLS-ROMEO-01`, `FW-JULIET-06`, `FW-BRAVO-02`) gently elevate as a radial cryptographic verification wave sweeps across the horizon. This proposal **proposes a new logo motif**—the **Bastion Octagon & Vault Aperture** (displayed in both dark and light variants)—replacing the "X" with a chamfered double-octagon perimeter and four inward-pointing locking callipers framing a central cryptographic diamond. It fits neXus because the platform runs inside an isolated, air-gapped bank network where pinned SSH/TLS identity gates and strict action-class boundaries protect critical financial infrastructure.

## Proposal 4 (`proposal-4.html`) — Quiet Authority
**Quiet Authority** adopts a minimal premium Swiss financial instrument aesthetic defined by `1px` architectural hairlines, generous breathing room, and tabular-numeral precision. The mood is understated, institutional, and timeless, pairing a distraction-free sign-in console with a horological SVG vernier alignment dial whose outer and inner calibrated rings slowly counter-rotate over a soft breathing aurora refraction to symbolize comparing configured intent against effective runtime truth. For the brand mark, this proposal **evolves the "X" motif** into the **Continuous Alignment Ribbon X** (shown in light and dark variants), sculpting the cross as two smooth interwoven paths that meet at a central axis within a refined Swiss neo-grotesque `neXus` wordmark. It fits neXus because operators managing a bank's firewall estate value clarity over noise, where every visual element reflects verified alignment between expected configuration and live operational state.
