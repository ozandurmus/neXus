Logo · on dark

Hex X. A new mark rather than an evolution: the X sits inside a hexagonal cell, the unit of the lattice, and its centre is left open, a deliberate gap where the two strokes would meet. The gap is the point of the product: neXus does not assume the crossing, it verifies it. The wordmark is NEXUS in wide-tracked capitals, which gives the identity an engineered, hardware-adjacent tone.

Logo · on light

On light the cell takes the primary blue and the strokes the product navy. The mark is single-weight and survives one-colour reproduction (a stamp, an engraved badge, a 16 px favicon) because the hexagon alone already identifies it.
