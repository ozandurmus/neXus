Logo · on light

Scribe X. A new mark that keeps only the letter: an X written with a broad nib, one hairline up-stroke and one heavy down-stroke, in a single ink. The weight contrast does the work the two colours used to do, so the mark survives engraving, embossing and a one-colour print, and never fights the status colours inside the product. The wordmark sets "neXus" in a classic old-style serif that ships with every operating system.

Logo · on dark

On dark the ink becomes paper. Nothing else changes, which is the point: one shape, two inks, no gradients, no tile. If a brand colour is wanted for the app icon, the heavy stroke can take the primary blue while the hairline stays ink.
