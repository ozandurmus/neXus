Logo · on light

Gate X. The current X kept as an X, but the two strokes now mean two things: the solid blue stroke is the rule, the dashed teal stroke is the traffic that meets it. The wordmark is the product name in a heavy lowercase sans, with the X of "nexus" drawn as the mark itself, so word and mark are one object. The navy tile stays, so it still reads as the same app in a browser tab.

Logo · on dark

On dark the tile inverts to the light surface tone and the strokes take the deeper product colours; in the wordmark the strokes brighten to the dark-mode primary and teal so they clear AA on #0f1216.
