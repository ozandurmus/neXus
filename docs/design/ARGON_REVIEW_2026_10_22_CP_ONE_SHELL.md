1. **Output framing safety (wrong parse vs. `UNKNOWN`):**

* **P1-1 — `FramedShell.clean()` deletes `\r` (`0x0D`) and `\b` (`0x08`) instead of applying terminal line-overwrite semantics, causing `SHELL_FRAMING_DESYNC` (`UNKNOWN`) or corrupted lines if readline/prompt/command emits mid-line `\r`.**
