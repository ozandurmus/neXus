# failover_readiness_assessment — HA Readiness / Failover-Safety Assessment

## summary

Read-only per-vendor preflight battery that scores whether an HA cluster is safe to fail over, and why not.

## why

The safety net and foundation for any controlled failover; valuable on its own as a VERIFY-plane capability.
