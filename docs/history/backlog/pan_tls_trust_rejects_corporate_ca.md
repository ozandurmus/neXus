# Palo Alto discovery cannot reach a Panorama fronted by a corporate CA certificate: without a pinned fingerprint the transport accepts only certificates carrying O=Palo Alto Networks

status: planned · target: PanXmlApiTransport certificate verification; EnvironmentPanTrustRuleResolver

Observed on the live deployment 2026-09-20 when the Product Owner added a Palo Alto device. The discovery run recorded FAILED with a transport failure whose class is that the presented certificate is not issued by or subject to O=Palo Alto Networks. The verification has two branches: if a pinned SHA-256 fingerprint is configured it is compared and that is the whole check, and otherwise the only accepted certificates are those whose issuer or subject contains O=Palo Alto Networks, which in practice means the factory certificate. An estate that puts a corporate certificate authority in front of Panorama therefore cannot be reached at all unless its fingerprint is pinned. The pin is supplied through the worker environment variable PAN_DISCOVERY_TRUST_PINNED_FINGERPRINT_SHA256, so the immediate unblock is a Product Owner trust decision plus one deployment value, not a code change. The durable gap is that AGENTS.md states production TLS requires trusted corporate CA verification, and this transport has no corporate CA path at all: it has a vendor-certificate rule and a per-endpoint pin, and nothing between them.
