# compliance_trend_reconstruction — 0.7.7 -- Compliance trend retro-fill (PAN baseline reconstruction)

## Summary

0.7.5 shipped the append-only compliance trend ledger with deliberate no-backfill; this is that deferred follow-up. Feasibility check found most of build_compliance_posture's inputs (alignment, CP config, assignment/waiver policy, CE.1 checks) are not versioned per historical CAS snapshot, so a full re-run is not possible -- put the scope trade-off to the product owner directly, who chose narrow/labeled reconstruction over dropping the build or a broader unlabeled approximation. New utils/compliance_trend_reconstruction.py mines stored PAN effective-running snapshots, single-linkage time-clusters them into synthetic checkpoints (CAS carries no run_id), and evaluates the ten deterministic DEFAULT_RULE_PACK baseline controls per entity through the exact same live evaluator dispatch a real checkpoint uses. Every record is stamped reconstructed=true / reconstruction_scope=pan_baseline_rule_pack_only. utils/compliance_history.py gains append_reconstructed (idempotent on run_id) and history_view's trend now always compares against the newest live record, never a reconstructed one, even when a reconstructed record is chronologically newest. New offline main.py --compliance-trend-reconstruct maintenance mode (no network, no credentials). static/app.js's sparkline renders reconstructed runs dashed/hollow, never sharing a solid segment with a live run.
