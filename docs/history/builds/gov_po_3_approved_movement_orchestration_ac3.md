## Summary

Produces and freezes docs/design/GOV_PO_3_APPROVED_MOVEMENT_ORCHESTRATION.md (a deterministic local orchestrator dispatching an approved movement to a separate claude -p engineer process in its own git worktree, tracked through development and serialized integration under the standing relay#13 merge authorization, with no manual message-carrying) and implements it: scripts/orchestrator.py (start/status/stop/merge-lock); .claude/nexus-engineer.settings.json + scripts/nexus_engineer_tool_gate.py (default-allow engineer profile, gated only at the privacy/push boundary and the merge-lock+re-validation boundary); scripts/nexus_po_tool_gate.py extended with the new orchestrator prefixes plus a boundary-safe prefix-matching fix applied across the whole allowlist; scripts/local_relay.py extended with a canonical-location resolver (NEXUS_CANONICAL_RELAY_DIR/NEXUS_RELAY_FILE) and a real cross-platform shared append lock.
